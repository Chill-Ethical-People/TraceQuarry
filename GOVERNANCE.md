# TraceQuarry Governance

This document defines how the official TraceQuarry upstream project is
stewarded. It does not limit the rights to use, modify, or redistribute the
software under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

## Project Steward

Kensho is TraceQuarry's creator, original copyright owner, and project steward.
Chill Ethical People is the project's organizational home and maintainer
community.

For the official upstream project, the project steward has final authority over:

- the TraceQuarry project identity and official use of its marks;
- designation of official repositories, packages, builds, and releases;
- appointment, scope, and removal of maintainer and release permissions;
- acceptance of material changes to licensing, governance, security posture, or
  project direction; and
- designation of a successor project steward through an explicit written
  record.

Repository permissions, organization membership, maintainer status, review
authority, or release access are delegated operational roles. They do not by
themselves transfer copyright, creator status, trademark rights, or project
ownership.

## Maintainers

Maintainers may triage issues, review and merge changes, maintain detection
content, publish releases, and administer project infrastructure within the
scope delegated by the project steward. Maintainers are expected to preserve
TraceQuarry's local-first DFIR purpose, source traceability, cautious evidentiary
language, security controls, and public contribution standards.

Material governance, licensing, ownership, trademark, or official-release
changes require project-steward approval. Code-owner review rules identify the
current approval path in the repository.

## Contributors

Contributors retain copyright in their individual contributions unless a
separate written assignment says otherwise. Accepted contributions are licensed
under Apache-2.0 and must satisfy the Developer Certificate of Origin in `DCO`.
The DCO records a contributor's authority to submit a change; it is not a
copyright assignment.

## Official And Unofficial Distributions

Only repositories, packages, builds, and releases designated by the project
steward are official TraceQuarry distributions. Forks and modified distributions
remain subject to `TRADEMARKS.md` and must not imply official status or
endorsement.

## Changes To Governance

Governance changes should be proposed through a pull request so the rationale
and review record remain public. A governance change becomes effective for the
official project only when accepted by the project steward.

Questions about stewardship or delegated authority may be sent to
contact@chillethicalpeople.com.
